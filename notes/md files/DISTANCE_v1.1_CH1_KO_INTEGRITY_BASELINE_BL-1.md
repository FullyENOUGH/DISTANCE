## 2. Fixed Chapter Structure

### 1.1 가장 성공적인 착각

**English:** The Most Successful Illusion\
**Status:** BASELINE\
**Primary payload:** Time and space are extraordinarily successful
observational/normalizing frameworks, but descriptive and predictive
success does not automatically establish explanatory priority.\
**Preserved decision:** The previously identified
manuscript-control/meta-writing passage near the ending remains deleted.
The section closes on the question of whether change can still be
described when time and space are moved behind the explanatory starting
point.

### 1.2 가짜 데스크톱

**English:** The Fake Desktop\
**Status:** BASELINE\
**Primary payload:** A representation can be accurate and operationally
useful without being identical to the underlying process it represents.\
**Boundary:** The desktop analogy is functional and epistemic; it does
not assert that spacetime is literally a computer interface.

### 1.3 변화가 시작되는 차이 --- Difference

**English:** Where Change Begins --- Difference\
**Status:** BASELINE --- SURGICALLY REVISED\
**Primary payload:** Difference as relational non-equivalence required
for distinguishable change.\
**Revision decision:** Energy Gap material was reduced to the minimum
distinction needed to establish that Difference is necessary but
insufficient. Detailed energetic significance remains owned by 1.4.\
**Closing boundary:** "같지 않다는 사실" and "물리적으로 해소되지 않은
상태" are not identical.

### 1.4 해소되지 않은 차이 --- Energy Gap

**English:** The Unresolved Difference --- Energy Gap\
**Status:** BASELINE\
**Primary payload:** Energetic/dynamical significance of Difference
within an effective physical relation.\
**Canon boundary:** Difference ≠ Energy Gap; Energy Gap ≠ Momentum.
Energy Gap is not written as automatically creating Momentum.

### 1.5 관계의 거리 --- Distance

**English:** The Distance of Relationships --- Distance\
**Status:** BASELINE --- SURGICALLY REVISED\
**Primary payload:** Effective/relational availability, distinguished
from ruler-measured geometric separation.\
**Revision decision:** Full Momentum directionality was removed from
section ownership. Only the minimum correspondence needed to prepare the
transition to Momentum remains.\
**Canon boundary:** Geometric convergence and effective-Distance change
are not automatically identical; effective Distance is not proposed as a
universal replacement physical quantity.

### 1.6 변화의 방향성 --- Momentum

**English:** The Directionality of Change --- Momentum\
**Status:** BASELINE --- SURGICALLY REVISED AND RE-CHECKED\
**Primary payload:** Momentum as observable directionality associated
with unresolved Distance; Momentum is always present; multiple Momentum
coexist; what changes is which Momentum becomes effective under the
present relational configuration.\
**Revision decision:** The earlier boat/trajectory development that
approached Chapter 3 observable-motion dynamics was removed. The section
now terminates within the foundational Momentum vocabulary.\
**Canon boundary:** Momentum ≠ force, velocity, motion, or substance.
Potential Momentum does not mean absent Momentum.\
**Required correspondence:** stronger effective Momentum ↔ shorter
effective Distance.

### 1.7 해소와 재구성 --- Equalization

**English:** Resolution and Reconfiguration --- Equalization\
**Status:** BASELINE\
**Primary payload:** Non-teleological Momentum resolution and continuing
relational reconfiguration.\
**Required direction:** shorter effective Distance → stronger effective
Momentum → stronger Equalization tendency; Momentum resolution →
corresponding effective Distance increase.\
**Prohibition:** Equalization does not cause Distance to shorten and is
not an agent, chooser, optimizer, or purposive process.

### 1.8 DISTANCE로 바라본 Universe

**English:** The Universe Through DISTANCE\
**Status:** BASELINE --- SUBSTANTIALLY REVISED AND RE-CHECKED\
**Primary payload:** Synthesis of the established vocabulary into a
single explanatory lens, returning to the opening time/space problem
without glossary-style re-teaching.\
**Revision decision:** Sequential re-definition of 1.3--1.7 was removed
as the organizing structure. The section now centers on the shift in
explanatory starting point: change is primary; time is an
observational/normalizing scale; geometric space remains a valid
descriptive framework without automatic explanatory priority.\
**Boundary:** No Chapter 2 cosmology preview, Chapter 3
observable-motion theory, Chapter 4 physical reinterpretation, or v2.0
ontology is introduced.
