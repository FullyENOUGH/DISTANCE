-   Difference → Energy Gap → Distance → Momentum → Equalization →
    Momentum resolution → effective Distance increase.
-   Momentum is always present.
-   larger/stronger effective Momentum ↔ shorter effective Distance.
-   Equalization does not cause Distance to shorten.
-   Momentum resolution increases corresponding effective Distance.
-   geometric Distance ≠ effective/relational Distance.
-   global/broader and local relational processes mutually constrain,
    modify, and reorganize one another.
-   local Difference/Distance/Momentum are genuine at their effective
    scale.
-   broader configuration is not reducible to an isolated sum of local
    processes.
-   local dynamics are not passive consequences, fragments, or residues
    of a prior Global Momentum.
-   Island formation is non-teleological.
-   v2.0 ontology remains excluded.
