## 1. Chapter role

**English chapter title:** The Maximization of Fairness and the Collision of Competition  
**Recommended Korean working title:** 공정성의 극대화와 경쟁의 충돌

Chapter 9 explains how social Islands form, how collective direction becomes power, why power concentrates, and how suppressed Momentum can be dispersed through democratic structures. It closes when expanded participation makes inherited exclusion harder to justify and turns fairness itself into contested Social Momentum.

Chapter 10 owns the next transformation. It does not ask again why fairness is demanded. It asks what fairness does once it begins to reorganize social pathways: access is opened, several meanings of equality diverge, separated tracks weaken, common measures arise, individuals become comparable, and competition spreads across the population. Formal symmetry then exposes conditions and burdens that the common rule cannot make symmetric merely by applying itself equally.

Chapter 11 owns the biological and reproductive asymmetry revealed at that boundary. Chapter 10 may identify pregnancy and childbirth as the remaining embodied Difference that formal social symmetry cannot erase. It must not yet explain fertility decline, calculate the asymmetric cost of a shared child, develop compensatory fairness, analyze the withdrawal of reproductive Momentum, or turn the remaining asymmetry into the full clean-window argument.

## 3. Argument progression

The controlling progression is:

**access reorganization → plural equalities → separated tracks → barrier removal → common measures → one comparable track → universalized competition → unequal conditions under equal rules → gender convergence → exposed reproductive asymmetry**

This is a logical reconstruction, not a claim that history proceeds through a single necessary sequence. Each relation can advance, stall, reverse, or coexist with older structures. Legal opening may precede material access in one setting and follow economic change in another. Education, property, political voice, labor participation, measurement, and family structure can reinforce or obstruct one another without one becoming the universal first cause.

## 6. Canon/WC-1.2 control for drafting

1. Access expansion or barrier removal must not be described simply as Distance becoming shorter. Equalization resolves a specific Momentum; as that Momentum is resolved, the corresponding effective Distance increases. The changed network may make other Differences and Momentum newly effective.
2. Common measurement is a relational compression device, not a neutral revelation of the whole person and not an ontological measure of human value.
3. The single competitive track is formally symmetric but relationally heterogeneous. Symmetry in rules does not establish identity in bodies, histories, resources, or burdens.
4. Fairness is neither a cosmic goal nor the automatic moral result of power dispersion. It is a revisable social structure for deciding which Differences may legitimately affect which pathways.
5. Barrier removal, measurement, competition, and gender convergence must be written as mutually conditioning and historically variable processes, not an intentional or inevitable one-way causal chain.
6. The chapter must preserve the achievements of expanded personhood and access while examining the pressures they generate. It must not convert the argument into nostalgia for inherited roles.
7. Each section follows phenomenon → puzzle/contradiction → limit of existing intuition → reasoning shift → DISTANCE discovery. Terms may name the result but may not substitute for the reasoning.
8. Default target is approximately 6,000 Korean characters ±10%, with long-form essay paragraphs, no internal micro-headings or list prose, no section previews, and no manuscript-management sentences.

## 7. Drafting order and local checks

Each section should be drafted in the recovered order, with a local PRE-CHECK against the immediately preceding section and the next section’s exclusive payload. After drafting, POST-CHECK must verify:

- substantive philosophy remains after removing DISTANCE-specific vocabulary;
- the section does not re-teach 9.10 or a preceding Chapter 10 section;
- the next section’s central contradiction has not already been resolved;
- no language implies that Equalization shortens Distance;
- relation-specific Momentum/Distance changes are preserved;
- historical movement is not rendered as teleological progress;
- prose density, paragraph length, and voice remain aligned with the Chapter 5 baseline.
