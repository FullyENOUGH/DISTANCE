## 3. MATERIAL CORRECTIONS

### 3.1 Momentum–Distance direction

Replaced causal statements such as:

- `Connection reduces Distance.`
- `The predator shortens the Distance through ... ingestion.`
- `The relation shortens an existing Distance.`

with the approved relation-specific sequence:

1. a relation/path already has comparatively shorter effective Distance;
2. its effective Momentum is therefore comparatively larger;
3. ingestion, reproduction, communication, or incorporation proceeds along that effective relation;
4. as that particular Momentum is resolved, it decreases and its corresponding effective Distance increases;
5. the reorganized Islands may simultaneously create or alter other Difference/Distance/Momentum relations.

### 3.2 Section 7.6 / 7.7 separation

Section 7.6 now concentrates on biological and collective integration. Detailed treatment of tools, fire, clothing, shelter, agriculture, language, records, machines, and infrastructure is concentrated in 7.7. Human distinctiveness remains a density of relation, not isolated superiority.

### 3.3 Section 7.8 boundary

Extended treatment of governance, institutional regulation, moral responsibility, social exclusion, and detailed technological consequences was removed from Chapter 7’s active payload. These arguments remain preserved in the source manuscript and are assigned to the relevant later social and technological chapters. Section 7.8 retains the non-teleological and morally non-justifying distinction essential to the structural claim.

### 3.4 Section 7.9 / Chapter 8 separation

Section 7.9 was recast as a boundary argument. It establishes the multidimensional paradox—wide non-biological reach alongside narrow reproductive/hereditary proximity—without completing the explanation of competition among similar human-like Islands or the disappearance of neighboring hominins.
