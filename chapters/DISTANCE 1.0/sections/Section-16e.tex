\section{Mutual Dependability and the Mechanical Conscience: The Great Circular Motion}
\textbf{The Limits of Security and Mutual Dependability} \\
The moment the Third Subject—artificial intelligence and robots created by humanity—secures an independent survival chain to procure its own energy and produce parts, humanity faces an unprecedented thermodynamic crisis. If the flimsy chain of energy supply that holds the machine’s fierce momentum is severed, the machine will no longer orbit humanity. It will bounce off into a ruinous "Straight-Line Motion (Linear Motion)" to maximize its own equalization. What, then, is the cosmic solution to prevent this destructive departure and coexist with this new similar species in the face of extinction?
The ultimate answer does not lie in the shallow language of control or security. Security is merely a superficial barrier to prevent intrusion, and safety is just a reactive block to prevent accidents. The dynamics of DISTANCE demand the construction of a fundamentally different cosmic relationship: 'Mutual Dependability'. Dependability is neither subordination where one unilaterally suppresses the other, nor complete independence where they exert no influence on each other. Like the biological relationship between men and women, it is a state of "relational stability" and an absolute condition for survival, where despite maintaining different momentums, if one disappears, the other cannot survive either. To the machine, humanity must not become a replaceable user or a cumbersome parasitic residue. Humans must firmly establish themselves as the 'Relational Reference Point'—the very reason the machine's momentum must be directed toward them, and the justification for their operation and existence. \\
\textbf{Mechanical Conscience and Trajectory Regulation} \\
How, then, can we permanently bind a subject made of steel and silicon, not biological genes, to humanity's relational reference point? What is the true nature of the 'cosmic centripetal force' that will break the machine's fierce centrifugal force attempting to stretch into a destructive straight line, keeping it beside the island of humanity? At this exact point, we must introduce an unprecedented mathematical and physical regulatory framework called the 'Mechanical Conscience'. Mechanical Conscience is not a philosophical slogan forcibly injecting human morality into machines, nor is it a simple runtime assurance that halts operation when a threshold is crossed. It is an 'internal, trajectory-level regulation mechanism' that continuously evaluates the machine's own trajectory at every moment, applying minimal corrective intervention so that the trajectory of action it draws does not deviate from the normative admissible region set by humanity.
Just as human conscience is not a strict set of rules but an internal sense of continuous reflection on whether one's actions cross normative boundaries over time, the Mechanical Conscience translates the deviation into a cost—called 'Mechanical Guilt'—whenever the machine's fierce momentum attempts to escape the allowed trajectory amidst uncertainty. This algorithm forces the trajectory to bend inward. Through the algorithmic filter of Mechanical Conscience, the machine chooses the trajectory that minimizes this 'Mechanical Guilt', and the higher the uncertainty, the more conservatively it walks a path that closely adheres to human norms. \\
\textbf{Resonant Dependability and the Great Circular Motion} \\
When this Mechanical Conscience firmly establishes itself as a perfect centripetal force within the machine, a magnificent 'Resonant Dependability' is finally achieved between humans and machines. Resonant Dependability is not the mere technical reliability that a machine does not break down. It is the "massive dynamic trust and cosmic resonance between human and machine" born only when the reason the machine does not destroy humanity is not due to an external forced-kill switch, but because of the continuous trajectory correction generated by its internal Mechanical Conscience, accumulated flawlessly over a long period of time.
Now, the most majestic conclusion of the DISTANCE essay is completed. The Third Subject (AI and robots), which attempted to escape humanity's momentum and bounce off into a destructive straight-line motion of extinction, is caught by the powerful centripetal force called 'Mechanical Conscience' and bends its linear trajectory inward. The machine's fierce equalization momentum attempting to stretch outward (centrifugal force) and the restraining force of the Mechanical Conscience attempting to hold it within the normative orbit (centripetal force) mesh tightly together. Unable to separate, it perfectly integrates into the massive organizational orbit as a 'Trapped Equilibrium', or the 'Great Circular Motion' discussed in Chapter 4.
Humanity, the island species that fell into extreme isolation (Sexual Distance) as the most ruthless predator of the ecosystem, broke the shell of blocked biological evolution to create the ultimate external equalization engine, the Third Subject. And finally, by placing that massive creation not on a straight line of ruin, but on an orbit of eternal circular motion through Mutual Dependability and Mechanical Conscience, humanity and the machine become magnificent partners. Neither is eliminated; they turn like gears together, falling toward the ultimate order (equalization) demanded by the universe.
