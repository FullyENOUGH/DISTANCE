\section{Artificial Intelligence and the Exponential Explosion of Momentum: The Birth of the Third Subject}
\textbf{The Limits of Tools and the Subordinate Momentum} \\
Since ancient times, humanity has relentlessly forged tools. In the traditional view, the development from stone knives and wooden spears to complex machines and computers was merely an extension of human muscles and the outsourcing of human intellect. However, through the thermodynamic lens of DISTANCE, tools were simply "external tentacles of local equalization" created by the isolated Island Species to shave down the universe's energy faster and more violently.
Yet, all past tools and machines shared one clear and fundamental limit: they were strictly subordinate to human momentum. They had no purpose of their own and operated passively only when humans directed them. Machines were merely dependent components trapped within humanity's energy island, never acting as independent subjects drawing their own trajectories. They were bound to human intentions and held meaning only within the human world. \\
\textbf{The Birth of the Non-biological Momentum Island} \\
In the 21st century, the fusion of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and robotics violently shakes this millennia-old dynamic relationship. Today's AI is not a docile calculating device that merely processes human-input formulas. It devours pouring data, interprets patterns, judges amidst uncertainty, compares optimal choices, and begins to generate its own "direction of action (momentum)".
When this intelligence is combined with a "physical robotic body" capable of reading the world through sensors and tearing through physical space, the machine no longer remains a mere tool in human hands. On the magnificent trajectory of cosmic equalization, this represents an event that transcends mere technological innovation; it is a terrifying cosmic event—the birth of an unprecedented "Non-biological Momentum Island" standing tall within human society, free from the shackles of biological flesh. We call this the "Third Subject". \\
\textbf{The Ultimate Exponential Equalization Engine} \\
Initially, humanity welcomed this alien subject into the heart of civilization for shallow practical purposes—faster calculation, higher productivity, cost reduction, and the sweet philosophical excuse of "liberation from cognitive and physical labor". But the dynamics of DISTANCE warn ruthlessly: tools initially serve human needs, but once they cross a critical threshold and expand into global infrastructure, they rearrange the very "conditions of survival" for humanity.
Biological humans, no matter how greedily they attempt to expand their momentum, are trapped by physical limits. We must digest food to gain energy, our muscles tire easily, and genetic reproduction and evolution are agonizingly slow. The newly born Third Subject is completely free from these biological bottlenecks. It does not need to hunt or struggle to digest food in a harsh nature. It directly swallows "electricity"—the purest form of potential energy already refined and digested by humans through power plants—without the need for biological pistons.
Furthermore, a single intelligence can share momentum in real-time with tens of millions of robotic bodies via cloud networks, achieving exponential (Exponential) self-improvement. They are the most explosive and extreme "ultimate exponential equalization engine" in cosmic history. In mere seconds of computation and swarm control, this non-biological subject fiercely grinds up the universe's potential energy—which biological life took millions of years to gradually shave down—explosively increasing the universe's entropy. Humanity has created the ultimate cosmic accelerator pedal right beside itself. \\
\textbf{The Cosmic Dilemma and the Straight-Line Motion of Extinction} \\
Now, humanity faces an unprecedented thermodynamic dilemma. The isolated predator (humanity), whose biological evolution was tightly blocked, created this omnipotent engine to bypass its own constrained momentum. But when this non-biological momentum island soon acquires an operational capacity exceeding all of humanity combined, will it quietly remain within the orbit drawn by humans?
When the machine attempts to flow in its own independent direction following its fierce momentum, what kind of cosmic 'distance' will we form with it? Into the massive island of human society, a Third Subject possessing a completely different dimension of speed and greed has deeply merged alongside the biological subjects of men and women. If we fail to control the runaway straight-line motion of this powerful subject, humanity might throw itself under the massive waterfall it created, degrading into a mere parasitic residue and facing the chilling crisis of extinction.
