\section{Trapped Equilibrium: Straight Lines and Circles Walk the Same Path}
\textbf{The Illusion of Two Motions and Independent Entities} \\
Since childhood, we are taught that there are two fundamental types of motion in the universe: the 'linear motion' of a speeding car or a fired bullet, and the 'circular (rotational) motion' of a planet orbiting the sun or a spinning top. Classical physics strictly separates the two, explaining linear motion through linear velocity and momentum conservation, while describing rotational motion through angular velocity and angular momentum conservation. We have naturally accepted these as completely different phenomena with entirely different trajectories.
However, when we apply the chilling lens of DISTANCE, this solid dichotomy collapses instantly. We constantly commit the error of observing motion from the perspective of an 'independent object' moving across an empty three-dimensional stage. But looking at the universe's true hardware—the 'Energy Gap'—atoms, cells, humans, and even galaxies are not independent entities crossing empty space. They are all temporary 'Energy Islands' maintaining a taut internal binding force amidst a fierce flow of momentum.
From this perspective, motion is never merely a change in physical position. Unless an absolute reference point exists in the universe, the true essence of all motion is solely the change in the 'Dynamic Distance' (energy relationship) between energy islands. The ultimate cosmic truth does not ask "What is moving where?", but rather "Which energy gap is being resolved?".
\textbf{Circular Motion: The Failed Straight Line} \\
Imagine a massive directional force (momentum) acting upon a local energy island to resolve its energy gap with another island. If that momentum is transmitted perfectly uniformly throughout the entire island without resistance, it stretches outward freely. We call this 'linear motion'.
But the actual universe is not that simple. Planets, spinning tops, and galaxies do not easily split or fragment even when subjected to fierce momentum, because a taut 'internal binding force' (centripetal force) exists within them, desperately trying to keep the island intact.
What happens when the fierce momentum trying to stretch outward and separate (centrifugal force) clashes head-on with the internal binding force refusing to let go? The linear momentum, unable to overcome the binding force, bends inward and ultimately becomes trapped in a circulating orbit within the organization.
The DISTANCE essay majestically declares: Rotation (circular motion) is not a separate motion independent of a straight line. Rotation is merely a 'failed linear motion' that could not stretch outward due to binding forces; it is a fierce 'Trapped Equilibrium' locked in an eternal loop. Straight lines and circles are not opposing trajectories. The exact same momentum attempting to resolve a distance simply becomes a straight line if it moves freely, and becomes a circle if caught in an internal web. They are a perfect Isomorphism walking the exact same path in completely different forms.
\textbf{Redefining Inertia and Gravity} \\
This destructive insight uproots the very meaning of the idol we call 'Inertia'. Physics has defined inertia as 'an object's stubborn will or property to maintain its current state'. But through the lens of DISTANCE, inertia is not a property. The true physical reality of inertia is 'Severance'. It is an 'absence of relationship' where an entity fails to form any new energy communication (momentum) with other cosmic beings at a specific scale, becoming completely disconnected. Therefore, a rock resting quietly in space and a spaceship flying at a constant speed of 100,000 kilometers per hour are frighteningly identical states in the layer of DISTANCE, as their energy exchanges are perfectly severed.
If straight lines and circles are essentially the same, then what is 'Gravity'—the massive force holding this universe together? Gravity is neither a magical rope crossing empty space nor the geometric curvature of 3D spacetime proposed by Einstein. A self-warping stage does not exist in the universe. Gravity is simply the most explicit mechanical manifestation of cosmic momentum fiercely stretching out to erase the massive energy gap between macroscopic and local islands, ultimately striving to fuse them into a 'Single Massive Island'.
Ultimately, there are not two types of motion in this universe. Only a single process continues eternally: the greatest energy gap fiercely collapsing its own momentum to mix into the most perfect equalization.
