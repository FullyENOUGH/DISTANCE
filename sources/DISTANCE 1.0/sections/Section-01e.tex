\section{Time Does Not Flow: The Fake Desktop of Spacetime}

\textbf{The Illusion of Time and Space} \\
We firmly believe that time flows. I wake up in the morning, check the clock, move according to my appointments, and welcome the night as the day ends. The smartphone screen displays time down to the second, and the car dashboard tells me how fast I am moving across physical space. We calculate the distance from home to school, from the Earth to the moon, and from the solar system to other galaxies, accepting time and space as the most absolute and fundamental framework sustaining our lives and the universe.
However, looking through the profound lens of DISTANCE, we must ask the most fundamental question: Does 'time' truly exist first, and do events occur within that transparent river of time? Does a pre-existing empty stage called 'space' exist where objects draw their trajectories?
We watch the second hand of a wall clock click and declare that "time has passed". Yet, the actual physical truth occurring in that exact moment is merely that the mechanical devices and gears inside the clock operated, energy was transferred, and the position of the hands changed due to that physical force. Time is not the fundamental cause of cosmic change; it is merely a 'scale' that human perception attaches retroactively after a change has occurred.
Space is an identical illusion. When we say an object has moved from here to there, we believe it has crossed an empty three-dimensional stage. However, at the level of actual hardware, only certain cosmic energy states have shifted, and the mutual network of relationships between entities has changed. The limited human brain and our mathematical tools have merely translated this complex, multidimensional change of energy into a visual and physical format called 'distance'. Ultimately, what we blindly trust as space is not the fundamental reality of the universe itself, but merely a 'coordinate system of perception' arbitrarily rendered by the brain to process the explosive results of fierce energy interactions.\\
\textbf{The Fake Desktop: Humanity's Survival Interface} \\
The metaphor that most perfectly exposes this deceptive cognitive process is the 'computer desktop interface'. We see yellow folders, document files, and a trash can on the monitor screen. If we drag an unnecessary file into the trash can, it is deleted. But if we open the computer's casing and look into the physical world inside, there are no folders, files, or trash cans. There is only the relentless switching of logic circuits and the fierce flow of complex electrical signals. Folders and trash cans are merely 'symbolic interfaces' (fake desktops) overlaid so humans can intuitively control that complex, non-linear electrical reality.
What we believe to be the absolute truth—time and space—is exactly this kind of survival desktop. The human brain did not evolve as a noble organ to pierce the mysteries of the Big Bang or the profound hardware of the universe. Our brains evolved harshly to quickly predict the parabolic trajectory of a thrown stone in a barren savanna, intuitively gauge the distance from an attacking predator, and calculate how fast we must move to reach a vital oasis. The fact that time and space feel so breathtakingly real and absolute to us does not prove they are the true hardware of the universe; it is a painful testament to just how fatally successful humanity's 'survival interface' has been.\\
\textbf{The Linear Ruler and the Normalization Ratio Function} \\ 
This absolute illusion is also the root of the greatest contradiction facing modern physics. General relativity, which deals with gravity in the macroscopic world, and quantum mechanics, which deals with the microscopic world, have achieved dazzling successes in their respective domains. However, ultimate attempts to integrate the two repeatedly fail, spitting out the error of mathematical infinity (Singularity). The reason is that we are still assembling equations while blindly trusting 'time and space' as the most fundamental vessels (absolute measures) containing the universe.
The actual hardware of the universe is a delicately fluctuating and complexly intertwined "non-linear sea of momentum". However, humans have forced the extremely stiff, derivative, and "linear measuring tools" called time and space over this dynamic sea to measure it. When we try to measure the depth and churning volume of the ocean with a 15-centimeter straight plastic ruler, at some point, the calculation will inevitably collapse.
When the calculation explodes and the ruler snaps, we should not blame it on a 'bug in the universe'. We must admit from the start that the linear ruler (spacetime) was a thoroughly inadequate tool. The geometric distortion or "curvature of spacetime" that modern physics has believed in is not a physical reality, but merely a massive epistemological error—a "distortion ratio"—generated by the mismatch between the linear tool and the non-linear reality.
To break through this limit, we must discard the derivative and linear variables of time, space, distance, and speed from our fundamental equations. Instead, we must derive a "Normalization Ratio Function" that mathematically tracks the distortion ratio between linear observation and non-linear reality, based solely on the momentum generated by the energy differences between entities. By replacing our broken ruler with this absolute mathematical tool, we can finally strip away the illusion of spacetime and prepare to face the true hardware of the universe.
