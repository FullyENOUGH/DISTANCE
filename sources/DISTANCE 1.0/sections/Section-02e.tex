\section{The Universe's True Hardware: Energy Gap and Equalization}
\textbf{The Engine of Change: The Energy Gap} \\
If we clear away the flimsy, survival-driven desktop of spacetime, what is the true hardware of the universe revealed on the empty stage? The DISTANCE philosophy majestically declares that at its root lies the 'Energy Gap'. Every event, movement, and change in the universe is triggered solely by differences in energy—the massive drop between high and low energy, or the extreme imbalance between compressed potential and released states.
Consider a massive rock resting precariously on a mountain peak. To human eyes, the rock seems perfectly still, but a massive difference in potential energy, an invisible gap, fiercely boils between the rock and the deep valley floor below. When the rock finally rolls down, humans apply their artificial scales to describe it as having moved a certain distance over a certain time. However, the true fundamental cause of that majestic fall is neither time passing nor space opening, but solely the Energy Gap and the massive momentum attempting to resolve that gap.
Valley water plunges down a mountain not because time passes, but because a fatal energy gap called a slope exists. When a waterfall grinds down rocks and casts a rainbow, science tries to precisely measure the density of the spray or the speed of erosion. Yet, the most chilling and absolute thermodynamic fact dominating that valley is simply that water is falling from a high place to a low place, and energy is fiercely resolving its own gap.\\
\textbf{Overturning Entropy: The Trajectory of Equalization} \\ 
To fully grasp this dynamic hardware, we must ruthlessly overturn the traditional meaning of 'Entropy'. We are often taught that entropy is the inevitable increase of disorder or chaos, using shallow, human-centric metaphors like a messy room or a crumbling sandcastle. However, through the profound lens of DISTANCE, entropy is not mere chaos. It is the fierce trajectory of 'Equalization'. It is the relentless process of heading toward a state of perfect flatness where all useful energy gaps are erased and no differences remain.
Think of a completely cooled pot on a stove. When parts of the pot are hot and others are cold, a fierce flow of heat occurs due to that clear energy gap. But when enough time passes and all parts of the pot reach exactly the same temperature, heat flow completely stops. Is this state a violent chaos where everything is mixed? Absolutely not. This quiet state, where flows vanish and events cease, is precisely when entropy is maximized. It is a perfect halt where momentum disappears and time itself loses its meaning.
When energy gaps are erased, flows stop, and the ultimate destination of the universe is this perfect equalization where momentum becomes zero.
